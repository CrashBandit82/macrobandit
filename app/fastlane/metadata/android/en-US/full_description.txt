MacroBandit is a free and ad-free macro and calorie tracker. It works offline and without an account: all data stays on your device.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Quick entry like "200g milk 3.5" – amount detected, calories and macros calculated instantly</li>
<li>7,140 basic foods offline from the German food database (BLS 4.0, Max Rubner-Institut)</li>
<li>Barcode scanner (zxing, no Google services) and online search – product data from Open Food Facts, on request only</li>
<li>Own foods and meal templates, applied with one tap</li>
<li>Weekly and monthly statistics, weight tracker with history</li>
<li>TDEE calculator and personal macro goals</li>
<li>Backup and restore as a file, CSV export</li>
<li>German and English, light and dark theme</li>
</ul>
